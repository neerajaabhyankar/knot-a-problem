Knot Editor

Paint, but for knots in 3D space.

Getting around

Left-drag orbits, right-drag pans, scroll zooms. F frames everything you've drawn. Clicking without dragging selects a strand.

Drawing

Draw stays armed until the strand is finished. Your stroke lands on the draw plane — the faint grid facing you, through the orbit centre. Hold ⇧ for a straight line. Finish by ending a stroke back where you started, which closes the loop, or press Enter to leave it open.

Over and under: lift the pen

Draw the way you would on paper. Keep the pen down and the strand passes over; lift the pen across a crossing and the gap is bridged by an arc that dips under. So a trefoil is three strokes with three lifts.

Leave a crossing ambiguous — both passes drawn with the pen down — and those crossings are made alternating instead, so the result is still a genuine knot. The status line tells you when that happened.

Linking two loops

  1. Draw a loop.
  2. Orbit about 90°. It goes edge-on. Arm Draw and two pink dots appear where it pierces the draw plane.
  3. Draw a second loop around one pink dot. That's a Hopf link.

Editing

Select an open strand and its ends get grab handles — drag one to keep drawing from there. Bring it round to the other end and the strand ties into a loop.

Erase rubs away part of the strands you've selected, and only those. A bite out of a loop makes it an open arc; a rub through the middle of an arc splits it in two. [ and ] change the size.

Everything is undoable with ⌘Z.
